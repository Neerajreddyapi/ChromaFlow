# Code of Conduct

Chroma Flow is a small, friendly project. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else connected to the project) to feel welcome and safe.

## Our standards

**Do:**

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before jumping to conclusions.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, opinions and ways of working.

**Don't:**

- Harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone.
- Use sexual language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Make discriminatory jokes or comments about identity, appearance or background.
- Share anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, troll, or deliberately derail conversations.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, and to public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **pingdrop.support@gmail.com**. All reports are read and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. The response depends on how serious the behaviour is: from a private warning to a permanent ban.
