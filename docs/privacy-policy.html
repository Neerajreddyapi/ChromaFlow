Chroma Flow — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Contact pingdrop.support@gmail.com

Chroma Flow is a fluid art app for Android, developed by Neeraj Reddy. You touch the screen and colorful fluid swirls under your finger. This policy explains what the app does with your information: in short, nothing leaves your phone.

1. No data is collected

Chroma Flow does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

2. No internet access

The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. Everything runs locally on your phone.

3. What is stored on your phone

Your settings (chosen style, colors, slider values and switches) are saved inside the app's private storage on your phone so they are still there next time you open it. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes them. "Reset everything" in the app puts them back to the defaults.

4. Permissions

Chroma Flow requests no Android permissions. The optional "Tilt to pour" feature reads your phone's motion sensor to make the fluid flow downhill; Android does not require a permission for this, and the readings are used only in the moment to move the fluid. They are never recorded or sent anywhere.

5. Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Email pingdrop.support@gmail.com.
